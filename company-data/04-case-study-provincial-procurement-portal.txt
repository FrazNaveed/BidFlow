CASE STUDY — Provincial E-Procurement Portal Enhancement

Client: Provincial government agency (Sindh — name available under NDA)
Sector: Public sector / digital governance
Contract value: PKR 22.5 million
Duration: 11 months (2023–2024)
Outcome: Successful go-live; 99.7% uptime in first 6 months post-launch

Scope
Enhance existing e-procurement portal: vendor registration, tender publishing, bid submission workflow, evaluation committee module, and integration with existing financial management system (FMS). Required compliance with government data hosting guidelines and accessibility standards.

Meridian approach
• Requirements traceability matrix (RTM) mapped 847 RFP clauses to design and test cases
• Agile sprints with fortnightly demos to procurement directorate
• Security: OWASP Top 10 remediation, penetration test by third party, role-based access control
• Hybrid hosting: application tier on approved cloud; database on-premises as mandated

Key deliverables
• Enhanced portal (React front-end, Node.js API, PostgreSQL)
• FMS integration via REST APIs (vendor master, PO creation)
• Admin training for 45 procurement officers (3 cohorts)
• Operations runbook and SLA monitoring dashboard

Results
• Tender cycle time reduced from average 74 days to 41 days (directorate metrics)
• 100% of mandatory compliance clauses verified at UAT
• Evaluation score on contract performance review: 92/100

Certifications applied
ISO 27001 controls for access management and encryption at rest; ISO 9001 documented change control.

Relevance
IT services, public procurement, integration, security, training, and SLA-backed support tenders.
