CASE STUDY — Detailed Business Plan Development for National Incubator Program

Client: Karandaz (representing a cohort of 12 incubated ventures)
Sector: Entrepreneurship / economic development
Contract value: PKR 8.4 million
Duration: 6 months (2025)
Outcome: All 12 business plans accepted by investment committee; 9 ventures received follow-on funding

Scope
Karandaz required investor-ready detailed business plans for ventures spanning agri-tech, fintech, and light manufacturing. Each plan had to meet a 39-section template including executive summary, market analysis, operations plan, staffing, risk matrix, and 5-year financials with DCF and break-even analysis.

Meridian approach
• Assigned a lead economist and sector analyst per venture cluster
• Conducted 2-day facilitated workshops with each founding team
• Built bottom-up revenue models tied to verifiable market data (PSLM, industry reports)
• Peer review by independent reviewer before submission to Karandaz technical panel
• Produced English and Urdu executive summaries

Key deliverables
• 12 complete business plan documents (150–220 pages each, PDF and editable Word)
• Consolidated assumptions register and sensitivity tables
• Presentation decks for investment committee (12 × 15 slides)
• Final knowledge transfer session for Karandaz staff on plan quality standards

Results
• Zero rejections on first submission (previously ~30% resubmission rate with other vendors)
• Average committee score: 87/100 on technical completeness rubric
• Client reference letter on file (Ref: KAR/BPD/2025/14)

Relevance to similar tenders
Mandatory experience in business plan development, financial modeling, incubator/DFI contexts, bilingual reporting, and fixed-fee multi-project delivery.
