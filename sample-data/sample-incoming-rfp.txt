REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Cloud Security Platform — Enterprise License
Issued by: State Department of Technology
RFP Number: RFP-2026-CS-0042

SUBMISSION DEADLINE: March 15, 2026
Questions due: February 28, 2026

BUDGET CEILING: $500,000 (not to exceed)
Contract term: 3 years with two 1-year renewal options

EVALUATION CRITERIA:
Technical Approach — 40%
Security & Compliance — 30%
Past Performance — 20%
Price — 10%

MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS:

1. The vendor shall demonstrate SOC 2 Type II compliance with an audit report no older than 12 months.

2. Describe your data encryption approach for data at rest and in transit. All data must be encrypted using industry-standard algorithms.

3. The solution must support SAML 2.0 or OIDC single sign-on integration with Azure AD and Okta.

4. Vendor must maintain 99.9% uptime SLA for production services with documented incident response procedures.

5. Provide your incident response plan including notification timelines for data breaches affecting customer data.

6. Confirm GDPR compliance for any processing of EU citizen data.

7. Describe your disaster recovery and business continuity capabilities including RPO and RTO targets.

8. List all sub-processors and their security certifications.

9. Provide three references from government or enterprise clients in the last 3 years.

10. Submit pricing broken down by year for the 3-year base period.
